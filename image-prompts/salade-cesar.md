Use a warm home-kitchen tabletop, three-quarter view, centered dish and safe crop margins. Render believable food with fine ink contours, hand-painted watercolor texture and soft cel shading in CookiGram's warm contemporary style. No text, logo, watermark, packaging, people, hands, unrelated garnish or photorealism.
