# Prompt d'illustration — Magret de canard rosé au miel & romarin

Create an entirely original horizontal 16:9 manga-inspired culinary illustration of “Magret de canard rosé au miel & romarin”, based only on the written recipe and not on an existing photograph. Show a clean rustic wooden board or warm stoneware platter presenting a roasted duck breast fanned out in neat thick slices. The duck meat exhibits a completely uniform succulent pink core from center to edge, capped by a thin, beautifully rendered golden-brown crisp diamond-crosshatch scored skin. A rich, glossy syrupy honey and rosemary pan jus is delicately drizzled over the slices. Fresh aromatic rosemary sprigs and roasted garlic cloves garnish the board. In the soft background, a precision sous-vide immersion circulator in a water bath on the kitchen counter. Three-quarter angle view, centered dish and safe crop margins. Render believable food with fine ink contours, hand-painted watercolor texture and soft cel shading in CookiGram's warm contemporary style. No text, logo, watermark, packaging, people, or photorealism.
