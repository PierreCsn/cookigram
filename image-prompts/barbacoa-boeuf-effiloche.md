# Prompt d'illustration — Barbacoa de bœuf effiloché aux piments chipotle & agrumes

Create an entirely original horizontal 16:9 manga-inspired culinary illustration of “Barbacoa de bœuf effiloché aux piments chipotle & agrumes”, based only on the written recipe and not on an existing photograph. Show a rustic cast-iron platter filled with steaming, glossy shredded braised beef glistening in a rich dark red-brown chipotle adobo sauce. In the soft background, an electric multicooker pressure cooker (Instant Pot) with its stainless steel lid off on a warm wooden kitchen countertop. Three-quarter angle view, centered dish and safe crop margins. Render believable food with fine ink contours, hand-painted watercolor texture and soft cel shading in CookiGram's warm contemporary style. No text, logo, watermark, packaging, people, or photorealism.
