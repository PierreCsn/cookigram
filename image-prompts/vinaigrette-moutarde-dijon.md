# Prompt d'illustration — Vinaigrette moutarde de Dijon

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: horizontal 16:9 hero illustration for a responsive recipe website
Primary request: Create an entirely original manga-inspired culinary illustration of "Vinaigrette moutarde de Dijon" based only on this written dish description, not on any existing photograph.
Scene/backdrop: A simple warm home-kitchen tabletop, softly suggested and uncluttered.
Subject: A small light ceramic bowl centered in the scene, filled with a smooth, glossy pale golden-yellow Dijon mustard vinaigrette. The emulsion should look silky and slightly thick, with subtle highlights that communicate a sauce that coats a spoon. A clean whisk rests beside the bowl, and a simple closed glass storage jar is softly visible in the background, matching the recipe's preparation and conservation. No salad leaves, herbs, pepper, lemon, or other unsupported garnish.
Style/medium: polished contemporary manga food illustration, elegant fine ink contours, hand-painted watercolour texture and soft cel shading, appetizing but believable sauce textures, a distinctive original CookiGram visual identity; generic manga aesthetic, not imitating any named artist, studio, series or existing artwork.
Composition/framing: landscape 16:9, three-quarter view at about 45 degrees, bowl centred with generous safe crop margins for mobile and desktop cards, no human figures.
Lighting/mood: warm late-afternoon kitchen light, welcoming and homely.
Colour palette: pale mustard yellow, warm cream ceramic, muted olive and honey tones, soft brown tabletop, gentle amber highlights derived from Dijon mustard, olive oil and white wine vinegar.
Constraints: visually faithful to the recipe; fully original composition; suitable as a website recipe illustration; no text in the image.
Avoid: text, letters, logos, brand marks, watermarks, product packaging, characters, faces, hands, unsupported garnishes, third-party image imitation, photorealism, distorted utensils, excessive props.

Art-direction note: the selected illustration shows the vinaigrette in a ceramic bowl with a spoon, whisk and closed storage jar, preserving the recipe's emulsion and conservation cues.
